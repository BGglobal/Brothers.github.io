FAQ

Good questions.

Have something else to ask? Talk to us ↗

Who can join the creator network?

Creators and page owners on Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn and other platforms. We review every profile for niche, audience quality, consistency and brand fit, not just follower count.

How does BMG work with brands?

Share a brief with your objective, audience and budget. We propose a creator mix and campaign plan, then handle negotiation, contracts, production, approvals, going live and the final report.

How is pricing structured?

It depends on scope, creators and deliverables. Whatever the structure, the commercial terms are documented and shared with you before any work starts.

How soon will I hear back?

You'll get a confirmation email as soon as you submit. Our team reviews every submission and usually replies within 3–5 working days.

How do creator payments work?

Each step is recorded: invoice issued, payment received from the brand, then creator settlement. You can see where your payment is at every stage.
